# v0.2.0: Wardrobe catalog and a new phone-first panel

**New:** a redesigned Laundry panel, built for phones first.
- Four tabs: Home, Closet, Add and Settings (bottom bar on phones, a top bar on wide screens).
- Home shows a tile for each closet and wash bin with counts and photos, plus drying and missing items. Tap a tile to see those garments.
- Closet shows every garment as a photo card, with search and filter chips. Use Select to move, change or delete several at once.
- Add walks you through it: tap the new tag on any station and the form fills in by itself, then pick the owner, wash bin and dry type with big buttons.
- Settings shows your bins, dry types and whether each station is online.
- Changes appear right away, without refreshing. It follows Home Assistant's light and dark mode.

**New:** Wardrobe catalog (optional, off by default). Turn it on in the integration options.
- Up to 8 photos per garment, taken straight from your phone's camera. Photos are shrunk and their hidden details (like location) are removed.
- Brand, size, store, purchase date and price for each garment.
- Paste a product link to fill in the name, brand, price and photos. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
- With an AI Task entity chosen, garments are described from their photos: type, colours, sleeve, fit, material, seasons and more. Anything you change by hand stays as you set it.
- Filter the closet by any of these details, for example "navy long sleeve".
- When describing, photos and details are sent to the AI provider you chose.

**Change:** stored garment data moves to a new format. A copy of your old data is kept in `.storage/laundry_hub.v2.backup`, in case you need to go back to v0.1.2.

The tap station firmware didn't change, so there's no need to reflash the stations.

Not yet tested on real hardware.
