# v0.1.0: First build of Laundry Hub

**New:** track tagged clothes with NFC tap stations and light up the right laundry bin.
- Tap a garment at the laundry station: it goes into the wash and its wash bin lights up. Tap it again after 5 minutes: it moves to drying and its dry zone lights up.
- Tap it at a closet station to mark it put away.
- Unknown tags are remembered, and you get a notification that opens the Enroll page.
- Garments not seen for 14 days (adjustable) are listed as missing. They are never deleted.

**New:** everything is set up in Home Assistant.
- Wash bins and dry types are entries you add, edit, reorder and remove under Laundry Hub. Each has a name, a colour and an optional light.
- A bin that garments still use can't be removed. Move the garments first with Bulk reassign.

**New:** the Laundry sidebar panel (admins only), with Garments, Enroll and Status pages.

**New:** sensors for each closet, in the wash, drying, missing, unknown tags and each wash bin, plus a tap event.

**New:** firmware for the M5Stack AtomS3 Lite tap stations, with the Unit NFC reader or the RFID2 reader as a fallback. The station LED shows green for a known tag, blue for unknown, red for an error and white for moved, and blinks amber if Home Assistant doesn't answer.

Not yet tested on real hardware.
