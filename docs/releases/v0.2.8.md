# v0.2.8: Garments without a tag

**New:** a garment no longer needs an NFC tag. In Add, choose "No tag (wardrobe only)" next to tapping a tag, or use "New garment from this" on a purchase card. Untagged garments get the same photos, details, AI description and fit as any other, with a small "No tag" badge and a "Tagged / No tag" filter in Closet.

**New:** an untagged garment's laundry state is set by hand, with "To laundry" and "Clean / put away" on the garment. Laundry Hub never guesses where an untagged garment is, never calls it missing, and leaves it out of the closet and laundry-bin counts. A state set by hand a long time ago is treated as less certain.

**New:** "Wore it" on every garment, tagged or not, with Undo on the message for 10 seconds. Laundry Hub remembers each wear, ready for outfit suggestions in a later version; for now it shows how often and when a garment was last worn.

**New:** "Attach tag" on a garment links the next new tag tapped at any station or with the app, within 2 minutes. To take a tag from another garment, "Move tag here" lists your tagged garments by name and photo and moves it after one confirmation; no tag IDs to type (typing one is still there under Advanced). "Replace tag" (for a lost tag) and "Remove tag" (back to no tag, keeping its history) are on every tagged garment.

**New:** when a new tag is tapped, its notification and the Add page also offer "Link to an untagged garment…", with the most likely garments first.

**New:** with App access set to Household, family members can add untagged garments for themselves, set their laundry state, and attach, replace or remove tags on their own garments.

**Change:** "Not clothing" on a purchase card now keeps its photo, so Restore brings it back without downloading it again. Archived and dismissed cards, and their photos, are removed a year after they were archived or dismissed; Settings → Purchase emails says so. Medication and health items are still removed straight away.

**Fix:** the red "[object Object]" message that often appeared when the Laundry Hub panel opened, mostly in the Home Assistant iPhone app. Errors now always read as plain words ("Lost connection to Home Assistant", "Laundry Hub is still starting"). If Home Assistant is still connecting when the panel opens, it keeps showing "Loading…" and tries again on its own instead of showing an error.

**New:** Purchases is easier to find. The Home page shows a "Purchases · N to review" card with a few pictures whenever new purchases are waiting, and the Closet tab shows the count. Tapping the card opens Purchases. Links can open it directly too (?tab=closet&view=purchases).

The tap station firmware didn't change, so there's no need to reflash the stations.

Not yet tested on real hardware.
