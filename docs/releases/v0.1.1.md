# v0.1.1: Drying clothes stay drying

**Change:** tapping a drying garment at the laundry station in auto mode now keeps it drying and lights its dry zone again, however long it has been.
- To wash it again, switch the station to sort mode and tap.
- Garments in the wash work as before: a second tap within 5 minutes lights the same bin again, a tap after that moves them to drying.

Not yet tested on real hardware.
