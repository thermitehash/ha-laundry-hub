# v0.2.6: One card per item, and cards for each person

**Fix:** a one-time cleanup tidies the purchase cards made before v0.2.5. Cards that aren't clothing are removed with their photos, duplicates of the same item become one card, and names like "Pants - Black / M / Tall" are split into colour, size and cut. Cards you already matched to a garment aren't touched. Settings shows the result once: "Cleaned up: X non-clothing removed, Y duplicates grouped".

**New:** one card per item. The confirmation, shipping and delivery emails of one order no longer make separate cards or add up: they fill in the same card. Buying the same item again (another order, or more than two weeks later) adds to the card's quantity ("×2"), and each purchase gets its own line in the card's history with the date, store, order number and price.

**New:** the quantity has − and + buttons. A quantity you set stays put when more emails arrive, until you choose "Use the emails' count".

**New:** Split pulls one purchase out into its own card, and Merge combines two cards.

**New:** a card for several units can be matched to one garment for all of them, or one unit per garment (for example two of the same dress for two people). It stays in Purchases until every unit is matched or dismissed.

**New:** assign purchase cards to a household member: "Assign to…" on a card, or select several and assign them together. Filter Purchases by All, Unassigned or person. After you give two similar items (the same size line such as "Kids", or the same product line) to one person, new similar cards show "Suggested: <name>"; nothing is assigned until you tap it.

**Change:** with App access set to Household, family members now see the purchase cards assigned to them (in the app) and can match them to their own garments, dismiss them or mark them Not clothing. They still never see other cards or store settings.

**New:** Amalli Talli is a built-in store. Its orders come through Shopify without the store's name in the subject, so they're recognised by the sender's name or the shop's own Shopify address. For any Shopify store you add by name, Laundry Hub now learns its Shopify address from the first order, so later emails that don't name the store are still found. Another shop using the same name is refused.

**Change:** with "Clothing only" on, items that aren't clothing never get a card, a name or a photo. Amazon Pharmacy emails are never read, and medication or health items are never stored in any order. Store sales emails ("Deals up to 40% off") make no card.

**New:** brand is read from order emails again, and the printed cut ("slim", "tall") shows on cards. When you match a card, a cut like slim or relaxed fills in the garment's fit, and tall, petite, plus or maternity go to its size. A brand you set yourself is never replaced. A price printed only as a line total is divided by the quantity.

The tap station firmware didn't change, so there's no need to reflash the stations.

Not yet tested on real hardware.
