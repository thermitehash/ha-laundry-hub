# v0.2.1: Purchase inbox from order emails

**New:** purchase cards from your order emails (optional, needs Wardrobe and an AI Task entity).
- In Laundry Hub → Configure → Purchase emails, enter your Gmail address and an app password (Google Account → Security → App passwords). Other email providers work with Generic IMAP.
- Pick the stores you shop at from a list of 37. Nothing is chosen for you, and each store shows how many of its emails arrived in the last 90 days. You can add any other store's sender domain.
- Order emails from those stores become purchase cards: item, store, date, price, product link and photo. Shipping and delivery emails update the card instead of adding a new one.
- When you add a garment, Laundry Hub asks "Is this from a recent order?". Tap the card to fill in the name, brand, size, purchase details and photo.
- Closet → Purchases lists the cards waiting to be matched. Match one to an existing garment, dismiss it, or mark it "Not clothing" so that product won't come back.
- A garment that came from an order shows "From your Old Navy order, Nov 22" with a link to the product.
- New sensor: `sensor.laundry_hub_unmatched_purchases`. New action: `laundry_hub.check_purchase_emails`.
- Privacy: Laundry Hub only reads mail (it never sends, deletes, moves or marks anything) and only from the stores you chose. The text of those emails is sent to the AI provider behind your AI Task entity. Email text, subjects and addresses are never stored or logged.
- If Google stops accepting the password, a repair in Settings → Repairs lets you enter a new one.

**Fix:** iPhone photos in HEIC format are converted to JPEG on your phone before uploading. If your browser can't read them, you'll see how to switch the camera to "Most Compatible".

**Fix:** product-link imports and order-email photos are protected against a DNS trick that could have reached your local network.

**Fix:** "Laundry Hub" no longer shows up in Home Assistant's media browser. AI descriptions still see your garment photos.

**Change:** stored data gains a place for purchase cards. v0.2.0 can still read it, so going back needs no restore.

The tap station firmware didn't change, so there's no need to reflash the stations.

Not yet tested on real hardware.
