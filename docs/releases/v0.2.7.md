# v0.2.7: A gentler, safer cleanup of old purchase cards

**Change:** the one-time cleanup of purchase cards made before v0.2.5 no longer deletes cards that aren't clothing. It marks them "Not clothing" and dismisses them, keeping their photos, so Restore in Purchases → Archived brings any of them back. Medication and health items are still deleted. Settings shows the result once: "Cleaned up: X marked not clothing (restorable), Y health items removed, Z duplicates grouped".

**Fix:** if the AI isn't available when the cleanup runs, nothing is changed and it isn't counted as done. It tries again at each of the next 3 checks. If the AI is still unavailable, Settings shows "Cleanup skipped: AI unavailable — use Reclassify old cards", and the Reclassify old cards button runs it again whenever you like.

This replaces the cleanup in v0.2.6, which wasn't installed anywhere, so on updating from v0.2.5 or earlier the v0.2.7 cleanup is the one that runs.

The tap station firmware didn't change, so there's no need to reflash the stations.

Not yet tested on real hardware.
