# v0.2.5: Purchase emails read in full again

**Fix:** order emails are read properly again with Claude (Anthropic) as the AI. Claude had been turning down the request as "too complex", so every email fell back to a bare version: items came in without colour, size or price, the import sometimes failed, and non-clothing items were added as cards. Each email is now read in two small steps: first the order and the product names, then the details of up to ten products at a time.

**Fix:** with "Clothing only" on, an item is only added once the AI has said whether it's clothing. If even that can't be read, the email counts as a failed read and is tried again later, instead of becoming a card that might be a lamp.

**Change:** purchase cards now also keep the cut or fit printed in the email (for example "slim" or "tall").

**Change:** AI garment descriptions are asked in two smaller parts for the same reason. Fields the AI isn't sure about are left empty, as before.

**Change:** if the AI turns a request down as too complex, the log says so once per check, with the size of the request, and a smaller request is used.

The tap station firmware didn't change, so there's no need to reflash the stations.

Not yet tested on real hardware.
